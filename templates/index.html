How it works

1. You draw

Sketch a digit on a canvas with your mouse or finger. It's automatically downsampled to the 28×28 grayscale format the model was trained on.

2. The network runs

Three convolutional layers with batch normalization extract shapes and curves, then three fully-connected layers turn them into a guess.

3. Confidence fills up

Softmax turns the network's output into a probability for every digit. Ten liquid gauges fill live — the fullest one is the network's best guess.
